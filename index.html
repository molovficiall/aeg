Our priorities

There are many factors that impact the economic landscape in Virginia, and we are committed to helping a movement for change.

  • Lower business taxes

    Reduce tax rates on small businesses so owners keep more of their earnings.

  • Early Entrepreneurship

    Promote entrepreneurship and experience for young individuals interested in starting their own businesses.

  • Diverse Business

    Support policies that end unnecessary regulations and barriers for business owners.

About the Group

The American Economic Group is a policy advocacy organization focused on Virginia economic policy. Our work centers on a single conviction: when small businesses keep more of what they earn, they invest, hire, and strengthen their communities.

We welcome input from members and citizens alike. Tell us what you think.
