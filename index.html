Policy Priorities

How we help small business thrive

Too many viable businesses fail under the weight of taxes and compliance costs. We advocate policy that lets owners reinvest, hire, and stay open.

Lower Business Taxes

Reduce federal tax rates on small businesses so owners keep more of their earnings.

Keep More, Reinvest More

Expand deductions, expensing, and credits that let businesses invest in equipment, workers, and growth.

Prevent Bankruptcy

Relief and tax treatment that helps struggling businesses recover rather than collapse.

Simpler Tax Compliance

A simpler code and fewer filing burdens so owners spend time running businesses, not paperwork.

Family & Succession Relief

Protect family-owned businesses from tax burdens that force sales or closures.

Regulatory Relief

Cut compliance costs that fall hardest on the smallest employers.
